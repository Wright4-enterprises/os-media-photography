About

Behind the lens

Perth-based automotive photography, shot by someone who's actually part of the scene.

Who's shooting

OS Media is run by Owen Smith, an automotive photographer based in Perth, Western Australia. Owen shoots car meets, private builds, and dealership content across the Perth metro area, with a focus on getting the details that actually matter to someone who's put time and money into their car — the stance, the lines, the paint, the little touches most photographers skip past.

What started as photographing cars at local meets has grown into private shoots and content work for owners and dealerships who want their car represented properly, not just snapped on a phone.

OS · PERTH WA

How it works

Every shoot starts with the car — what it is, what makes it worth shooting, and where the light's going to do it justice. Locations are scouted ahead of time, shoots are timed around golden hour where possible, and turnaround on edited photos is fast because nobody likes waiting weeks for their own car.
