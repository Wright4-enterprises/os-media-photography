Privacy Policy

How OS Media handles the information you share.

Last updated: August 2026
1. Information we collect +

When you get in touch about a shoot, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and details about your car or the shoot you're enquiring about. If you book through our booking page, we also collect the date, time, and details you submit through that form.

2. How we use your information +
  • To respond to enquiries and arrange shoots
  • To communicate with you about bookings, changes, or delivery of photos
  • To deliver edited photos to you after a shoot

We don't sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Photos and content +

Photos taken during a shoot may be used for OS Media's own portfolio, website, and social media unless you specifically request otherwise when booking. If you'd like your shoot kept private, let us know before or during booking.

4. Data storage +

Enquiry and booking details are stored securely and only used for the purposes described above. Photos are stored using third-party hosting and storage providers as part of running this website.

5. Your rights +

You can ask to see what information we hold about you, or request that it be corrected or deleted, by contacting us directly.

6. Contact +

Questions about this policy can be sent to osmedia1@outlook.com.
